Major Pipeline Transformations

01

Grayscale Conversion

Eliminates chrominance variation to focus solely on luminance intensity, reducing computational dimensionality.

02

CLAHE Equalization

Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization enhances local contrast to expose faint license plate boundaries.

03

Bilateral Filtering

Smooths flat background textures while strictly preserving sharp character edges, preventing blurring across glyphs.

04

Canny Edge Detection

Calculates multi-directional intensity gradients to isolate structural candidate contours of the rectangular plate frame.

05

Otsu Thresholding

Calculates the optimal global binarization threshold via inter-class variance maximization for clean black/white masks.

06

Morphological Closing

Applies rectangular structuring element closing to bridge fragmented character outlines and consolidate license plate blobs.

07

Geometric Scoring

Evaluates candidate bounding boxes using aspect ratio, vertical gradient texture, and contour density heuristics.

08

Topological Character OCR

Segments individual glyphs and matches against normalized synthetic font templates with topological hole-count verification.
